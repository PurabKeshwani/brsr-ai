# Stage 4 Final Audit — Greenwashing / Vagueness Detection

**Audit date:** 2026-10-18  
**Status:** Rule-based text-vagueness prototype frozen; exploratory comparison complete; human validation unavailable.  
**Frozen version:** `stage4-rule-detector-2026-10-18`

## Executive summary

The evaluated Stage 4 component is a frozen, rule-based **text-vagueness subdetector** that combines lexical hedging/evasion checks with semantic similarity. It is not a trained final classifier. The current results do not validate the integrated four-signal auditor: claim-vs-figure and assurance-gap checks were not evaluated by these text-only comparisons. The XGBoost meta-learner remains experimental; its previously stated F1 target of 0.94–0.96 was not achieved.

Two comparisons are available:

1. Development batches 1+2, N=80: F1 **0.7436**. These examples informed detector development, and whether their labels were blind to model flags is unconfirmed.
2. Batch 3, N=70: F1 **0.6885** against AI-generated text-only annotations. This is an exploratory AI-label comparison, not human-held-out validation or gold-standard performance.

There are no independent human Batch 3 ratings, adjudication, or Cohen's kappa. Therefore Stage 4 is **not human-validated**. Do not present either result as human-held-out performance or as performance of the complete four-signal auditor.

## Frozen detector

The fixed detector combines the lexicon and semantic signals. Its frozen configuration is described in [`stage4_threshold_freeze.md`](stage4_threshold_freeze.md), and source hashes are stored in [`stage4_detector_freeze.json`](../../data/validation/stage4_detector_freeze.json).

The principal semantic thresholds are:

- With KPI/numeric evidence: vague similarity ≥0.58 and at least 0.15 above quantified similarity.
- Without KPI evidence: vague similarity ≥0.42 and above quantified similarity.
- Long-text lexicon hedging: for text over 80 words, hedging density ≥0.005.
- URL/evasion rule: a matching evasion signal flags the segment.

The thresholds were frozen before the N=80 recomputation and before Batch 3 annotation. Keep the engine frozen if these results are reported; tuning against either evaluated set would invalidate the comparison.

## Historical figures and claims to avoid

- Old engine, reported N=30: F1=0.880. Historical development figure.
- Old engine, N=80: F1=0.732; 15 FP and 7 FN were reported.
- Fixed engine, previously reported F1=0.923: N is uncertain, likely 30; do not describe as N=80 or held-out.
- Fixed engine tuning run, N=30: F1=1.000; in-sample threshold tuning, not a validation result.
- XGBoost F1 target 0.94–0.96: **not achieved**; XGBoost remains experimental.

User-reported implementation fixes include the currency regex false positive on “shareholders”, MW/GW unit recognition, statutory-entity terms (e.g., POSH/ICC/ETP/ZLD), and bare-URL evasion handling. These are implementation fixes, not evidence of measured performance gains.

## Scope and limitations

- Both current evaluations call `analyze_combined_vagueness()` and cover the lexicon + semantic vagueness subdetector only.
- Neither evaluation assesses claim-vs-figure or assurance-gap checks, nor the full integrated four-signal auditor.
- Batch 1+2 label blinding remains unconfirmed.
- Batch 3 AI-label comparison cannot replace independent human labeling, adjudication, or inter-rater reliability.
- No 95% confidence interval is available for the AI-label comparison; report it as a descriptive point estimate with N and provenance.
