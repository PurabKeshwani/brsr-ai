# Supplementary result tables

Prepared: 2026-10-22. These files retain the complete saved outputs behind the compact manuscript tables. No model inference or training was performed.

- **S1:** all 94 financial-service companies, every score component, reconstructed latest-filing end date, and saved sector percentile. The original score CSV omitted filing IDs; dates follow the existing latest-filing-by-CIN reconstruction and are not independently verified export-to-filing links.
- **S2:** all 72 sector groups, including all 25 groups pooled in the manuscript, with company counts, means, medians, ranges, and standard deviations. Source: data/analysis/sector_summary.csv.
- **S3:** all 16 filing records for eight paired-year companies, with filing IDs, reporting labels, and every score component. Source: data/analysis/year_wise_scores.csv. The available-case cohort is descriptive and does not establish a market-wide trend.
- **S4:** the earlier Pharmaceuticals example, comprising the four highest and three lowest saved scores within the recorded sector. Components are joined by CIN from transparency_scores.csv and sector_benchmarks.csv. Sector labels reflect the saved implementation, including applicability and classification limitations.

The original source CSVs remain the evidence sources. The score-band and pooled-row summaries are descriptive arithmetic rather than new predictive results.
