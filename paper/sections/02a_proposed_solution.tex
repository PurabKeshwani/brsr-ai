\section{Proposed Solution}
\label{sec:proposed}

GRASP connects a company's written claims with the information reported in the same BRSR report. Text segments and reported facts are stored under the same company and reporting period, providing shared data for classification, review checks, and scoring. An analyst can move from a company summary to the original disclosure behind a result, addressing the review problem described in Section~\ref{sec:introduction}. Model training takes place before report analysis. Report-derived weak labels train a domain-adapted BERT classifier to assign text segments to the nine reporting principles and produce principle probabilities. Word lists and sentence similarity flag potentially vague wording. Numerical and assurance checks create separate review records linked to the report evidence. The Transparency Score combines four components and reuses the vagueness detector to calculate Clarity, which contributes 20\% to the final score. Principle probabilities and numerical flags are separate outputs and do not enter the score calculation. Figure~\ref{fig:architecture} shows the workflow, and Section~\ref{sec:methodology} explains the calculations. Implementation requires collected reports, a database, pretrained language models, and computing resources for training and analysis. The service layer reuses the trained classifier and provides company records, principle summaries, review flags, scores, and sector comparisons through an application programming interface (API). System results cover one company, selected companies within a sector, the complete financial-sector group, and all recorded sectors. The same scoring rules compare reporting years when two annual reports are available. Section~\ref{sec:evaluation} reports classifier agreement with reference labels, vagueness-detection results, company-score comparisons, and checks that the service uses the intended model.\label{sec:proposed-end}\par
